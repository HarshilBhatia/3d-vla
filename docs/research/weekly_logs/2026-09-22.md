# Week ending 22 Sept 2026 — eval-run index

Index of every online-evaluation campaign whose result files were written in the
week ending 22 Sept 2026. Campaigns are grouped by theme, newest first within a
group. Percentages are unweighted mean task success over that arm's cells (task x
calibration), recomputed from the final `results_*.json` files, excluding
`.progress.json` and `.manifest.json` --- they
are pointers, not decision numbers; the per-condition tables live in
[docs/research/results/README.md](../results/README.md).

Roots (all absolute on the cluster):

- `R20  = /grogu/datasets/hbhatia/3dfa_online_eval_20rollouts`
- `R100 = /grogu/datasets/hbhatia/3dfa_online_eval_100rollouts`
- `RAUD = /grogu/datasets/hbhatia/3dfa_online_eval_audit`
- `RPRB = /grogu/datasets/hbhatia/3dfa_online_eval_probe`

Standing artifact map: [docs/research/results/artifact-index.md](../results/artifact-index.md).
Layout inside a campaign is `<arm>/<viewpoint regime>/<calibration id>/results_<task>.json`,
each paired with a `.manifest.json` recording checkpoint, runtime and seed.
