## Defaults changed this week

Both changes apply only to new runs. Old runs and checkpoints are unchanged.

- **History register off by default** (`video_deltam_history_register: false`).
  - It works on every path now: full-patch refine skips the readout, and Direct never had one.
  - Checkpoints saved without the key load with it on (`utils/config_migrations.py`).
  - The 13 pre-October refine configs pin `true`.
- **EMA on by default** (`use_ema: true`).
  - The 29 pre-October experiment configs pin `false`.
  - New runs ladder **EMA weights only** (`video_deltam_checkpoint_ladder_ema_v01.json`); raw ladders are skipped to save compute.
- **New flag `camera_adaln`** (`none | per_camera | pooled | static_pooled`). It is described in [architecture.md](../../handbook/model/architecture.md).

## Submission

- **Smoke tests** passed for all four arms on 1 GPU (jobs 3984986–3984989): 60 steps, a validation pass and an `interm_step` save. The EMA weights load through the eval rebuild path.
- **Single-node chains:** 4 GPUs, `--partition=shubhamlong,all`, 24 h links chained with `afterany`, `--requeue`. Each link resumes from the run's own `last.pth`.
- **Twin and race:** 1c also has a 4 nodes × 1 GPU twin (`scripts/multinode/config/exp1c_adaln_static.yaml`). `race_twins.sh` (log: `logs/train/race_<arm>.log`) keeps whichever form starts first. 1a's single-node job won its race straight away.
- **Bad node, grogu-3-30 (RTX 2080 Ti):** every job exits in 3 s with code 127 and writes nothing to stderr. The first 1a and 1c chains (3984990–3984992, 3984996–3984998) burned out there. Both chains were resubmitted with `--exclude=grogu-4-3,grogu-3-30`, and the pending links of 1b and 2 were updated to the same exclusion.

- **Slow nodes:** the single-node launcher has no GPU constraint, and 1b landed on Quadro RTX 6000 (Turing, fp32 fallback), at about 5× slower per step. Running jobs were left alone. Every pending link now excludes the Turing nodes (grogu-3-5/10/15/20/25/30, 4-18) and grogu-4-3. New single-node submissions should pass the same `--exclude`.
