## Defaults changed this week

Both changes apply only to new runs. Old runs and checkpoints are unchanged.

- **History register off by default** (`video_deltam_history_register: false`).
  - It works on every path now: full-patch refine skips the readout, and Direct never had one.
  - Checkpoints saved without the key load with it on (`utils/config_migrations.py`).
  - The 13 pre-October refine configs pin `true`.
- **EMA on by default** (`use_ema: true`).
  - The 29 pre-October experiment configs pin `false`.
  - New runs ladder **EMA weights only** (`video_deltam_checkpoint_ladder_ema_v01.json`); raw ladders are skipped to save compute.
- **New flag `camera_adaln`** (`none | per_camera | pooled | static_pooled`). It is described in [architecture.md](../../handbook/model/architecture.md).
