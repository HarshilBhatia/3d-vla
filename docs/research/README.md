# Research

What we are finding out. Everything here changes; the stable material is in
[`../handbook/`](../handbook/).

| Area | What it holds | Update when |
|---|---|---|
| [experiments/](experiments/) | One page per experiment, filed by the week it started | a job starts, a result lands, a caveat appears |
| [results/](results/) | Settled numbers worth citing | an experiment finishes and its numbers stop moving |
| [weekly_logs/](weekly_logs/) | Per-week narrative, status and decisions | continuously, during the week |
| [reproduce.md](reproduce.md) | Provenance for the Aug 2026 campaign | when a released artifact changes |
| [archive/](archive/) | Pre-Sept notes, **not canonical** | never |

The flow: an experiment page is born in `experiments/<week>/`, is narrated in
that week's log, and graduates its numbers to `results/` once they settle.
